Site Name

Digital Skills Resource Hub

This name was chosen because it clearly communicates the purpose of the platform — it is a hub (a central gathering point) for digital skills (tech and web development learning) and resources (courses, guides, and stories). It is broad enough to serve people globally while remaining focused on its core mission.

Optional domain consideration: digitalskillshub.org

Site Purpose

The Digital Skills Resource Hub is a three-page informational website designed to help beginners and career switchers discover and access quality tech and web development learning opportunities — no matter where they are in the world.

The site will provide:

  • A curated directory of online tech courses and learning platforms
  • Real success stories from people who have transitioned into the digital space
  • A resource guide with tools, tips, and pathways for beginners
  • A contact or newsletter signup form for users who want personalized guidance

The site aims to make the digital world feel more accessible, less intimidating, and achievable for anyone willing to learn — whether they are in Lagos, London, or Los Angeles.

Color Scheme

The color scheme uses teal and purple as primary and secondary colors — teal communicates trust, clarity, and technology; purple communicates creativity, ambition, and innovation. Together they create a modern, approachable, and professional feel suited for a digital learning platform.

Primary — Teal #00a8a8 Top header background, footer background, section heading bars, button backgrounds
Secondary — Purple #7c3aed Navigation bar background, CTA buttons, badges, text highlights, accents
Background — Cream #fefaea Page background, secondary section backgrounds, form backgrounds
Accent 1 — Light Teal #9ad3da Card backgrounds, scenario card backgrounds, borders, decorative dividers
Accent 2 — White #ffffff Card surfaces, form input fields, modal backgrounds
Text — Near Black #1a1a1a All body text across all backgrounds
